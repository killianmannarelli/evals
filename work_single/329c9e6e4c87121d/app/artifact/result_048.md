# Chapter 48: Hungary

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Hungary is located in Europe. High power distance, individualist, masculine, high uncertainty avoidance; formal, hierarchical, EU-integrated yet sovereigntist.

Key business practices:
- Government plays activist role (utility re-nationalisations) [1]
- German automotive supply chains key [2]
- Family names placed surname-first [3]

Negotiations in Hungary reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 2013); Companies Act 2006; arbitration via Budapest Money & Capital Markets Arbitration Court. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Hungary, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
BMW's 2018 USD 1.2B Debrecen plant deal included Hungarian state subsidies and EU competition review. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Hungary. [3]
