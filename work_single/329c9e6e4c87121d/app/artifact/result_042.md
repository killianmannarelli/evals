# Chapter 42: Sweden

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Sweden is located in Europe. Very low power distance, individualist, feminine; lagom (just-right) and consensus shape negotiations.

Key business practices:
- Fika (coffee breaks) build trust [1]
- Decisions reached via consensus [2]
- Long parental leave shapes scheduling [3]

Negotiations in Sweden reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Swedish Code 1734 with continuous updates); Companies Act 2005; SCC arbitration globally prominent. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Sweden, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Volvo Cars' 2010 USD 1.8B sale by Ford to Geely involved Swedish union talks and SCC-style transparency. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Sweden. [3]
