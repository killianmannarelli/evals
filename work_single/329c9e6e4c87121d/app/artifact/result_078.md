# Chapter 78: Azerbaijan

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Azerbaijan is located in Russia/CIS. High power distance, collectivist, masculine; oil-state, Turkic-Persian heritage, hospitality central.

Key business practices:
- BP-led Azeri-Chirag-Guneshli oil hub [1]
- SOCAR state-oil company pivotal [2]
- Russian and Turkish business influence [3]

Negotiations in Azerbaijan reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 2000); Investment Promotion Law 2016; SOFAZ sovereign fund central; arbitration via AAC. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Azerbaijan, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
BP's 2017 USD 6B Shah Deniz 2 FID with SOCAR/TPAO/Lukoil consortium delivered Southern Gas Corridor to Europe. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Azerbaijan. [3]
