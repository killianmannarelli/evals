# Chapter 55: Russia

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Russia is located in Russia/CIS. High power distance, collectivist, masculine, high uncertainty avoidance; vlast (power) and svyazi (connections) central.

Key business practices:
- State-led capitalism; Kremlin signals matter [1]
- Vodka toasts seal deals [2]
- Bureaucracy and risk of asset disputes [3]

Negotiations in Russia reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (1994/1996/2001/2006 Civil Code parts); Foreign Investment Law 1999; sanctions post-2022 complicate cross-border deals. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Russia, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Rosneft-BP's 2013 USD 55B TNK-BP deal showcased Russian state-strategic acquisition logic—and the later 2022 unwind illustrates sanction-era risk. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Russia. [3]

## Do's & Don'ts
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Use formal Russian patronymics (Ivan Ivanovich) | Use only first names |
| Accept vodka toasts thoughtfully | Refuse outright |
| Verify sanctions compliance rigorously | Assume past licences still valid |
| Build svyazi over multiple visits | Try to close on one trip |
| Bring small gifts (flowers, chocolates) | Give 13 flowers (unlucky) |
