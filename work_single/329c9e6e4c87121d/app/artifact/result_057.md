# Chapter 57: Kazakhstan

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Kazakhstan is located in Russia/CIS. High power distance, collectivist, moderately masculine; hospitality (qonaqjaylylyq) and clan ties strong.

Key business practices:
- Oil/gas dominated by Tengiz, Kashagan, Karachaganak [1]
- Kazakh language rising alongside Russian [2]
- AIFC modeled on DIFC for English-law finance [3]

Negotiations in Kazakhstan reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 1994/1999); AIFC offers common-law option for finance; arbitration via KAZIAC and AIFC Court. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Kazakhstan, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Tengizchevroil's 2016 USD 36.8B Future Growth Project FID with KazMunayGas/Chevron/ExxonMobil/Shell underscored complex Caspian-basin negotiations. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Kazakhstan. [3]
