# Chapter 51: Portugal

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Portugal is located in Europe. High power distance, collectivist, moderate masculinity, high uncertainty avoidance; warm, relationship-driven, Atlantic outlook.

Key business practices:
- Family conglomerates (Sonae, Jerónimo Martins) [1]
- Lusophone-Africa & Brazil ties significant [2]
- Long lunches and saudade-tinged rapport [3]

Negotiations in Portugal reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 1966); Companies Code 1986; arbitration via Lisbon Chamber. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Portugal, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
China Three Gorges's 2011 USD 3.5B EDP stake (later boosted in 2018) showed Portuguese privatisation negotiation under EU/IMF MoU. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Portugal. [3]
