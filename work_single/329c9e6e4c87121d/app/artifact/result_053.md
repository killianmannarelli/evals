# Chapter 53: Austria

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Austria is located in Europe. Very low power distance, individualist, masculine, high uncertainty avoidance; precise, formal, German-leaning.

Key business practices:
- Formal titles (Dr, Mag.) essential [1]
- Coffee-house culture for negotiations [2]
- Strong codetermination through works councils [3]

Negotiations in Austria reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (ABGB 1811); Companies Act 1965; arbitration via VIAC. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Austria, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Voestalpine's 2017 USD 1.4B Texas DRI plant negotiations bridged Austrian engineering with US energy/labour rules. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Austria. [3]

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
