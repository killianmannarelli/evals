# Chapter 56: Ukraine

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Ukraine is located in Russia/CIS. High power distance, collectivist, moderately masculine, high uncertainty avoidance; warm hospitality with Soviet-era formality.

Key business practices:
- Strong agribusiness (sunflower, grain) [1]
- Oligarchic ownership patterns shifting post-2022 [2]
- EU candidate status accelerating reforms [3]

Negotiations in Ukraine reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 2003); Companies Law 2017; arbitration via UAC ICAC; war-era EU candidacy reshapes regulatory direction. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Ukraine, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
DTEK-NJSC Naftogaz's 2017 USD 1B renewable-energy initiative anchored Ukraine's pre-war pivot to wind, with EBRD support. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Ukraine. [3]
