## Cultural Context
Bulgaria is located in Europe. High power distance, collectivist, feminine, very high uncertainty avoidance; Balkan-Orthodox cultural roots, EU-aligned.

Key business practices:
- Outsourcing and software hubs in Sofia [1]
- Head-shake/nod inverted from Western norms [2]
- Long greetings and small talk expected [3]

Negotiations in Bulgaria reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (Civil Code 1950, Commerce Act 1991); arbitration via Bulgarian Chamber of Commerce; EU rules apply. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Bulgaria, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Bulgarian Energy Holding's 2018 USD 1.3B IBEX gas-hub talks with Gazprom and EU showed Balkan energy-route negotiation. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Bulgaria. [3]
