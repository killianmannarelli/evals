# Chapter 47: Czech Republic

STATUS: complete

## Cultural Context
Czech Republic is located in Europe. Moderate power distance, individualist, masculine, high uncertainty avoidance; pragmatic, German-leaning business style.

Key business practices:
- Industrial heritage; Škoda VW-owned [1]
- Direct communication, formal titles [2]
- Beer culture aids relationship-building [3]

Negotiations in Czech Republic reward parties that combine technical preparation with cultural fluency. Locals expect counterparts to understand both the codified rules and the unwritten conventions of relationships, hospitality, and hierarchy that shape every meeting.

## Legal Framework
Civil law (recodified 2014); Business Corporations Act 2014; arbitration via Prague Arbitration Court. [1]

For cross-border transactions in Czech Republic, parties should pay attention to:
- Choice-of-law and dispute-resolution clauses (arbitration vs courts).
- Sector-specific licensing and screening requirements (e.g., foreign-investment review, antitrust, sanctions/export controls).
- Tax, transfer-pricing, and withholding obligations under local statute and applicable treaties. [2]

## Case Study
Volkswagen's 1991 USD 6B Škoda Auto acquisition began an evolving partnership and exemplifies CEE post-1989 industrial transformation deals. The episode is widely cited as a benchmark for how foreign and domestic parties balance commercial ambition with the cultural and institutional realities of Czech Republic. [3]
